# Reusable paper-reading note

[中文模板](reading-note.md)

Complete only what your actual reading depth supports, using your own words. Attach page, section, figure, or table locations to important conclusions. Distinguish not applicable, not reported, and not yet verified. Pass 1 judgments are provisional. For papers without quantitative results, replace the requested number with a central proposition and its supporting evidence.
